Our Mission

Telling Nepal's Golden Story

Sunaulo Sansar — meaning "Golden World" in Nepali — was born from a simple belief: every moment, every face, every mountain deserves to be seen in its full, radiant beauty.

Founded in 2016 by lead photographer Arjun Thapa in Kathmandu, we've grown from a one-man studio into a full creative team serving clients across Nepal, India, and internationally. From intimate family portraits to large-scale commercial campaigns, our commitment to quality and artistry remains unchanged.

We believe great photography is more than technical skill — it's empathy, patience, and the ability to become invisible so that authentic moments unfold naturally in front of your lens.
