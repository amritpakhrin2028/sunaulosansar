FAQ

Common Questions

Everything you need to know before booking your session.

How far in advance should I book? +
We recommend booking at least 4–8 weeks in advance for portrait sessions, and 3–6 months in advance for weddings or large events, especially during peak seasons (October–December). However, we do accommodate last-minute bookings subject to availability.
How long does it take to receive the final photos? +
Standard delivery is within 7–10 business days for portrait sessions, and 2–3 weeks for full-day events and weddings. Rush delivery (3–5 days) is available at an additional cost. You'll receive your photos via a private online gallery link.
Do you shoot outside of Kathmandu? +
Yes! We travel throughout Nepal — Pokhara, Chitwan, Lumbini, Mustang, and even Himalayan trek routes. Travel fees apply based on distance and duration. We also accept international assignments with advance notice.
What is your cancellation policy? +
A 30% deposit is required to secure your booking. Cancellations made more than 14 days before the shoot receive a full refund of the deposit. Cancellations within 14 days forfeit the deposit. We offer free rescheduling once with at least 7 days' notice.
Can we request specific editing styles? +
Absolutely. We have a signature editing style but are happy to adapt to your preferences — light and airy, dark and moody, film-inspired, or natural tones. We offer a complimentary style consultation before every session.
Do you offer videography as well? +
Yes! We offer cinematic wedding films, corporate videos, and short-form social media reels. Video packages can be added on to any photography package or booked standalone. Ask us about combined photo + video deals.
